Free and open source. It updates itself with Sparkle, and you can turn that off.

Why macOS asks first.

macOS asks because Screen Setter is free and isn't notarized by Apple, which needs a paid developer membership. To check that your download is the published one, compare its SHA-256 with the one on the release page:

shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Screen-Setter.dmg

Questions

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

Screen Setter is free and distributed outside the store, as a download from GitHub. Both the App Store and Apple's notarization need a paid developer membership, which is why macOS asks you to allow it once. The source code is on GitHub if you'd like to check it or build it yourself.

What does it send, and where?

No accounts, analytics or tracking. The only thing it asks the internet is whether there's a new version, and you can turn that off. Your layouts stay in one file on your Mac. Read the privacy policy.

Where is the app after I open it?

In the menu bar; it has no Dock icon. If macOS hides it to make room, open the app again to show its settings.

How do I remove it?

Turn off Open at Login, quit the app from its menu, and move it to the Trash. To remove your layouts too, delete the Screen Setter folder in ~/Library/Application Support.

Building it yourself? The source is on GitHub.
